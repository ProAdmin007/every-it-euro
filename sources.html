Legislation

  • 18 U.S. Code § 2713 - Required preservation and disclosure of communications and records

    Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School (US Code text) · Published: 2018-03-23 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Jurisdiction, Cloud

    CLOUD Act provision (Pub. L. 115-141, div. V) requiring providers to disclose data in their 'possession, custody, or control' regardless of whether it is located inside or outside the United States.

    https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2713

  • 50 U.S. Code § 1881a - Procedures for targeting certain persons outside the United States other than United States persons (FISA Section 702)

    Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School (US Code text) · Published: not stated · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Jurisdiction

    Authorises targeting of persons reasonably believed to be outside the United States to acquire foreign intelligence information, with compelled assistance from electronic communication service providers.

    https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/50/1881a

  • Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795 of 10 July 2023 pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2016/679 on the adequate level of protection of personal data under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework

    European Commission (Official Journal of the EU) · Published: 2023-07-10 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Jurisdiction, Regulation

    Current adequacy decision allowing personal data to flow to US companies certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

    https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2023/1795/oj

  • Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/482 of 31 January 2024 laying down rules for the application of Regulation (EU) 2019/881 as regards the adoption of the European Common Criteria-based cybersecurity certification scheme (EUCC)

    European Commission (Official Journal of the EU) · Published: 2024-02-07 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Regulation, Cybersecurity

    Adopts the first EU cybersecurity certification scheme (EUCC) for ICT products, applicable from 27 February 2025.

    https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2024/482/oj

  • Decision (EU) 2022/2481 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 December 2022 establishing the Digital Decade Policy Programme 2030

    European Parliament and Council of the EU (Official Journal of the EU) · Published: 2022-12-19 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Cloud, Sovereignty, Economy

    Sets 2030 targets including at least 10 000 climate-neutral highly secure edge nodes and at least 75% of EU enterprises taking up cloud, AI or big data.

    https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2022/2481/oj

  • Directive (EU) 2022/2555 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 December 2022 on measures for a high common level of cybersecurity across the Union (NIS 2 Directive)

    European Parliament and Council of the EU (Official Journal of the EU) · Published: 2022-12-27 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Regulation, Cybersecurity, Critical infrastructure

    Art. 21(2)(d) and 21(3) require essential and important entities to manage supply-chain security, and Art. 22 allows EU-level coordinated security risk assessments of critical ICT supply chains, including non-technical risk factors (recitals 90-91).

    https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj

  • Directive 2014/24/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 February 2014 on public procurement and repealing Directive 2004/18/EC

    European Parliament and Council of the EU (Official Journal of the EU) · Published: 2014-03-28 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Procurement, Regulation

    Art. 18(1) requires equal, non-discriminatory treatment of economic operators; Art. 25 requires no-less-favourable treatment of operators from GPA and other agreement signatories for covered procurement.

    https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/24/oj

  • Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data (General Data Protection Regulation)

    European Parliament and Council of the EU (Official Journal of the EU) · Published: 2016-05-04 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Regulation, Jurisdiction

    Art. 28(1) requires controllers to use only processors providing sufficient guarantees; Art. 48 states third-country court or authority orders to disclose personal data are only recognised or enforceable if based on an international agreement.

    https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj

  • Regulation (EU) 2019/881 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 April 2019 on ENISA (the European Union Agency for Cybersecurity) and on information and communications technology cybersecurity certification (Cybersecurity Act)

    European Parliament and Council of the EU (Official Journal of the EU) · Published: 2019-06-07 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Regulation, Cybersecurity

    Establishes ENISA's mandate and the EU cybersecurity certification framework; Art. 56(2) states certification is voluntary unless otherwise specified by Union or Member State law.

    https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/881/oj

  • Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 December 2022 on digital operational resilience for the financial sector (DORA)

    European Parliament and Council of the EU (Official Journal of the EU) · Published: 2022-12-27 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Regulation, Cybersecurity, Critical infrastructure, Jurisdiction

    Applies from 17 January 2025; Arts. 28-30 require an ICT third-party risk strategy, concentration-risk assessment, consideration of third-country law enforcement, and exit strategies; recital 82 states DORA imposes no data localisation obligation.

    https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj

  • Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 December 2023 on harmonised rules on fair access to and use of data (Data Act)

    European Parliament and Council of the EU (Official Journal of the EU) · Published: 2023-12-22 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Regulation, Cloud, Jurisdiction

    Applies from 12 September 2025; Arts. 23-31 require cloud providers to remove switching obstacles, Art. 29 bans switching charges from 12 January 2027, and Art. 32 requires measures against unlawful third-country governmental access to non-personal data.

    https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2854/oj

  • Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 October 2024 on horizontal cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements (Cyber Resilience Act)

    European Parliament and Council of the EU (Official Journal of the EU) · Published: 2024-11-20 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Regulation, Cybersecurity

    Art. 71 applies manufacturer reporting obligations (Art. 14) from 11 September 2026 and the Regulation as a whole from 11 December 2027; Art. 13(5) requires due diligence when integrating third-party components.

    https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj

Court ruling

  • Judgment of the Court (Grand Chamber) of 22 October 2024, Kolin Inşaat Turizm Sanayi ve Ticaret AȘ v Državna komisija za kontrolu postupaka javne nabave, Case C-652/22

    Court of Justice of the European Union · Published: 2024-10-22 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Procurement, Jurisdiction

    Holds that operators from third countries without a procurement agreement with the EU have no right to 'no less favourable' treatment and contracting entities may set differentiated arrangements for them.

    https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:62022CJ0652

  • Press Release No 106/25: Data Protection: the General Court dismisses an action for annulment of the new framework for the transfer of personal data between the European Union and the United States (Case T-553/23, Latombe v Commission)

    Court of Justice of the European Union (General Court) · Published: 2025-09-03 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Jurisdiction, Regulation

    The General Court upheld the DPF adequacy decision, noting the Commission must monitor US law continuously and may suspend, amend or repeal the decision if it changes.

    https://curia.europa.eu/jcms/upload/docs/application/pdf/2025-09/cp250106en.pdf

  • Press Release No 91/20: The Court of Justice invalidates Decision 2016/1250 on the adequacy of the protection provided by the EU-US Data Protection Shield (Judgment in Case C-311/18, Data Protection Commissioner v Facebook Ireland and Maximillian Schrems)

    Court of Justice of the European Union · Published: 2020-07-16 · Accessed: 2026-11-12 · Topics: Jurisdiction, Regulation

    Schrems II: Privacy Shield invalidated because US surveillance programmes were not limited to what is strictly necessary and lacked actionable redress; standard contractual clauses remain valid subject to case-by-case assessment.

    https://curia.europa.eu/jcms/upload/docs/application/pdf/2020-07/cp200091en.pdf
